About the Club

The Mankato Maker Club is a student group for anyone who likes to make things, from first-time beginners to experienced builders. We meet every week to work on small electronics, 3D printing, and woodworking projects.

No experience is required. All tools and safety training are provided.

What We Do

  • Weekly build nights
    • Soldering practice
    • Arduino and sensor projects
  • 3D printing workshops
  • Campus repair cafe
  • Guest talks from local engineers

How to Join

  1. Visit the events page and pick a meeting.
  2. Fill out the registration form.
  3. Attend your first build night.
  4. Pick a project and meet your team.

Featured Project

Solar-Powered Weather Station

Last semester our members built a weather station that logs temperature and humidity on the roof of the library. Come see the live data at our next open house.
